The Deep Learning Indaba is the annual gathering that strengthens African machine learning, and 2025's edition in Kigali was a milestone for the TRI AI community. TRI AI co-founder Tejumade Afonja served as General Chair for the event.

## What happened

Members of the TRI AI community attended workshops, presented research, and connected with practitioners from across the continent. The week brought together researchers, students, and industry practitioners for talks, tutorials, and practical sessions spanning the full breadth of modern machine learning.

## Why it matters

Events like the Indaba are where the relationships that power African AI get built. For TRI AI, showing up as part of this community — teaching, learning, and contributing research — is central to the "for Africa, built in the open" mission.
